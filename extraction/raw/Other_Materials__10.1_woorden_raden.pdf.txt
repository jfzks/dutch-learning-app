10.1 woorden raden 
Instructie: Choose a word from the table one by one. Don't say the word, but describe it as
best you can. The others have to guess the word as quickly as possible. You can use these
tips to define the word:
Give example sentences in which you can use the word
Give a synonym.
Give an antonym.
Use context and associations, for example the story of the dialogue.
Tell us what kind of word it is: noun, verbum, adjective?
Act out the word.
